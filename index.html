Allergens
Intolerances & sensitivities
Sprig checks each product's declared allergens and "may contain" warnings, then scans the ingredient list for hidden names: whey and casein for dairy, malt and spelt for gluten, and so on. The data is crowd-sourced and recipes change, so if you have a serious allergy, always check the pack itself.

How scoring works

Every product starts at 100. Points come off for signs of ultra-processing, and every deduction appears on the result screen with its reason. There are no hidden weights, and no brand can pay to change a score.
